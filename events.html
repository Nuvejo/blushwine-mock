Events

There’s always something on.

Our week has a rhythm — a signature Thursday, long Sunday lunches, and the odd special night when a winemaker or a flamenco guitarist takes over the room. Here’s what to look out for.

Every Thursday · Our signature night

Bring Your Own Wine Thursdays.

Every Thursday, the cellar takes a night off. Bring a bottle you love — something from the back of the rack, a holiday souvenir, a gift you’ve been saving — and we’ll pour it beside a menu built for sharing. It’s the most relaxed, most personal night of our week, and it’s become the one our regulars plan their fortnight around.

  1. 1Bring your bottleAny wine you like — red, white, sparkling, that dusty special occasion bottle. We’ll chill whites and open reds to breathe.
  2. 2$15 corkage per bottleProper glassware, a clean decant if it needs one, and no fuss. Bring one bottle or a few.
  3. 3We’ll match the foodTell Mateo what you’ve brought and he’ll steer the tapas and paella to suit it — that’s half the fun.
  4. 4Book aheadThursdays fill fast. Reserve a table so we can save you the good corner.

The rest of the calendar

Other things worth turning up for.

Dates move with the seasons and the guest list — the surest way to catch them is our newsletter or a follow on Instagram.

Last Friday monthly

The Winemaker’s Table

Once a month we hand a corner of the cellar to a visiting winemaker. A five-course tapas menu from Enzo, each plate matched to a pour, and the maker themselves telling the story behind every glass. Long, generous, and always a little bit late-running.

$120 per person · limited seats · booking essential

Every Sunday

Long Lunch Sundays

Sunday is for slowing down. From midday we run a relaxed shared feast — a rolling parade of tapas, a paella for the table, and a glass of something chilled. No set finish time; stay as long as the afternoon lets you.

$65 per person · walk-ins welcome, groups please book

Seasonal

Flamenco & Sherry Nights

A few times a year the tables get pushed back, a guitarist and dancer take the floor, and we pour our way through the sherry list — from bracing fino to treacly PX. Loud, warm, and unmistakably Spanish.

Ticketed · announced via newsletter & Instagram

Any time

Private Events & Group Bookings

Birthdays, engagements, work send-offs, or a quiet buy-out of the whole room. We’ll build a set tapas-and-paella menu around your group and match the wines — or open the floor for BYO. Tell us the occasion and we’ll take it from there.

From 8 guests · set menus from $70pp · call Luca to plan it
