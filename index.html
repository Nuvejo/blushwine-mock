Welcome

A neighbourhood wine bar with a Spanish heart.

Blush Wine started with a simple idea: honest Mediterranean food, a well-poured glass, and a room where the staff know your name by the second visit.

Owner Luca runs the floor, head chef Enzo runs the kitchen, and between them they’ve built the kind of place they always wished existed near the beach — unhurried, generous, and a little bit romantic. Come for one plate and a glass; stay for the whole long evening.

Our signature night

Bring Your Own Wine Thursdays.

Every Thursday, the cellar takes a night off. Bring a bottle you love — something from the back of the rack, a holiday souvenir, a gift you’ve been saving — and we’ll pour it beside a menu built for sharing.

  1. 1Bring your bottleAny wine you like. We’ll chill whites and open reds to breathe.
  2. 2$15 corkage per bottleProper glassware, a decant if it needs it, no fuss.
  3. 3We’ll match the foodTell Mateo what you’ve brought and he’ll steer the tapas.

The centrepiece

Paella, the way it should be — for the table.

Bomba rice, a proper socarrat, and whatever the coast and the markets are giving us that week.

From classic Valenciana to a jet-black squid ink and a garden-led vegetable pan, every paella is cooked to order and built to be shared. Give the kitchen forty minutes and pour something red while you wait.
