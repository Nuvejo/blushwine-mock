The Team

The people who’ll know your name.

A small crew who genuinely like each other — and who’d rather look after thirty guests properly than a hundred in a hurry.

Luca

Owner & Host

The face at the door and the reason it all feels personal. Luca has poured wine on three continents and can read a table in seconds — whether you want conversation or just to be left alone with the Rioja. Ask him for a recommendation; he lives for it.

Enzo

Head Chef

The quiet force at the pass. Enzo cooks the food he grew up eating — generous, unfussy, deeply seasoned — and treats the paella pan like a member of the family. He’s at the markets before most of us are awake, choosing what the menu becomes that day.

Mateo

Head Bartender & Sommelier

Our compass through the cellar. Mateo can find the right glass for any plate and any mood — a mineral Assyrtiko, a sun-baked Garnacha, a sherry you didn’t know you needed. On BYO Thursdays he’s the one who’ll taste what you brought and steer the table from there.

…and the rest of the family

A room runs on more than three people.

Behind Luca, Enzo and Mateo is a floor and kitchen crew who set the candles, learn your order, and keep the plates flowing. We hire for warmth first and train the rest — and when we’re looking for someone new, we say so out loud.
