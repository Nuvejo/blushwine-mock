We’re looking for a Sous Chef.

A rare spot has opened on Enzo’s pass. If you cook with care, love Mediterranean produce, and want a kitchen that closes on Mondays and actually means it — read on.

About the role

Blush Wine is a thirty-seat Spanish tapas and paella wine bar with big ambitions and no ego. As Sous Chef you’re Enzo’s right hand — running the pass when he’s off, leading prep and ordering, and holding the line on the standards that make regulars out of first-timers. It’s hands-on, produce-driven cooking with a genuinely tight, kind team.

What you’ll do

  • Run service on the pass and the paella station, and step up as head chef on Enzo’s days off
  • Lead daily prep, mise en place and station set-up to a consistent standard
  • Own ordering and stock with our local growers, fishers and suppliers
  • Help shape the seasonal menu — we want your ideas, not just your hands
  • Mentor junior chefs and keep the kitchen calm, clean and food-safe
  • Hit costs and minimise waste without cutting a single corner on quality

What we’re looking for

  • 2+ years in a senior kitchen role; Spanish, tapas or Mediterranean experience is a big plus
  • A real feel for produce and seasoning — you taste as you go
  • Calm under a Saturday-night rush and generous with a junior on a bad day
  • Trade qualification (Cert III/IV) or equivalent hands-on experience
  • Valid Australian work rights and a current Food Safety Supervisor certificate (or willingness to get one)

What we offer

  • Above-award pay, reviewed honestly and often
  • A two-day weekend that never moves — we’re closed Mondays and Tuesdays
  • Real creative input into the menu and specials
  • Staff meals, staff wine education with Mateo, and a proper team culture
  • A supportive owner (Luca) who’s on the floor, not in a spreadsheet
  • Room to grow as we do

How to apply

Send a short note about yourself and your CV to hello@blushwine.com.au with “Sous Chef” in the subject line. Tell us the last dish you cooked that you were proud of. We read every application and reply to everyone — even if it’s a no.

Not a chef but think you belong here? We’re always glad to hear from good floor and bar people too. Same address.
