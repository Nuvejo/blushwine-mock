Our story

The story behind Blush Wine

Luca spent over a decade working in hospitality across Europe before settling on the Sunshine Coast. He fell in love with the region's relaxed pace and tight-knit community — and knew it was the right place to open something truly personal. The idea for Blush Wine was simple: a place where good wine is never intimidating, where the food tells a story, and where regulars are known by name. Luca met head chef Enzo while both were working the summer season in a small coastal restaurant in southern Italy. Enzo's obsession with honest, ingredient-driven Mediterranean cooking matched Luca's vision perfectly. When Luca made the move to open Blush Wine, Enzo didn't hesitate to join him. Together they built a menu rooted in shared plates, bold flavours, and the kind of food that makes people linger at the table long after the last glass is poured.
