The Paella

Our centrepiece, cooked to order.

Every pan starts from scratch when you order it — bomba rice, a deep home-made stock, saffron, and a crackling socarrat at the base worth waiting for. Allow around forty minutes; pour something red and settle in. Paella is priced per person, minimum two.

The original · Signature

Paella Valenciana

The one that started it all. Free-range chicken, green and butter beans, rosemary and saffron over bomba rice — cooked the old Valencian way, no seafood, no shortcuts. If you’re new to paella, start here.

$38 pp · min 2

Surf & turf

Paella Mixta

Mar i muntanya — sea and mountain. Chicken, house chorizo and prawns together in one pan, smoky and generous. The crowd-pleaser of the group.

$42 pp · min 2

For the whole table

Paella de Marisco

An all-in seafood feast — prawns, mussels, clams and calamari over saffron rice, built big and set down in the middle for everyone to dig into. Our most-ordered pan on a Saturday night.

$46 pp · min 2

Chef’s favourite

Arroz Negro · Squid Ink

Jet-black and dramatic. Squid ink, tender calamari and a whisper of garlic aioli on the side. Enzo’s quiet obsession — order it once and it’s all you’ll want.

$44 pp · min 2

Plant-based · Vegan

Paella de Verduras

Proof that the garden pan is never an afterthought. Artichoke, capsicum, green beans, tomato and smoked paprika — every bit as deep and satisfying, and fully vegan.

$34 pp · min 2
